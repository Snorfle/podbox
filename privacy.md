# PodBox Privacy Policy

_Last updated: May 13, 2026_

PodBox is a podcast player for Xbox. We do not operate any servers and we do not collect, transmit, or sell personal information.

## Data the app handles locally on your console

- Your subscription list, playback position, and playlist queue.
- Credentials for private feeds you choose to add, stored in your console's secure credential store.
- Downloaded episode audio and artwork, stored in the app's local cache.

## Network requests

PodBox connects directly to podcast feed URLs, episode audio URLs, and the public Apple Music Search API to discover and play podcasts. We do not proxy or log this traffic.
